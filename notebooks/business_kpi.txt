The 8 KPIs the dashboard should display

Summary metrics cards (top of dashboard)
1. Total Revenue            -> SUM (Sales)
2. Total Profit             -> SUM (Profit)
3. Overall Profit Margin    -> (SUM(Profit) / SUM(Sales))
4. Total Orders             -> SUM (order id)

Trend Chart
5. Monthly revenue trend    -> Line chart, Sales by Month/Year

Breakdown Charts
6. Revenue by Region        -> Bar chart with profit color encoding
7. Revenue by Sub-Category  -> Horizontal bar, sorted descending

Relationship Chart
